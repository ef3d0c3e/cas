# Writing a cas-core native: three compile-time facts

- `Ball` is at `cas_num::ball::Ball`; it is not re-exported at the cas_num root (`use cas_num::Ball` fails).
- `View` is `#[non_exhaustive]`, but inside cas-core matches on it are exhaustive without a `_` arm; adding one is an unreachable-pattern lint under clippy -D warnings.
- `Eval::context()` returns `&'c Context` (the context's lifetime, not `ev`'s borrow): a native can hold `let ctx = ev.context();` and still call `ev.eval(..)` / `ev.warn(..)`.
