# C0111 — giac's univariate modgcd drops the gcd's integer content; gcd, heugcd keep it
tags: giac,gcd,modgcd,oracle,departure,normalisation · date: 2026-11-26 · phase/task: 1.8/T7 · origin: CI 45818464735; caseval probes

giac's gcd algorithms by name do not share gcd's normalisation. Univariate `modgcd` answers the primitive part with a
positive leading coefficient: `modgcd(4*x,6*x)` = `x` (gcd: `2*x`), `modgcd((-3*x+2)*4,(-3*x+2)*6)` = `3*x-2`, but a
constant gcd keeps its content (`modgcd(4,4*x)` = 4), and in two variables the content stays (`modgcd(4*x*y,6*x*y)` =
`2*x*y`). `heugcd`, `psrgcd`, `gcd` keep the content (psrgcd keeps the remainders' sign). A sweep that compares these
by name skips univariate modgcd draws whose `icontent(gcd(..))` is not 1 (`crates/cas-giac/tests/gcd_methods.rs`
random_modgcd); departure rows `modgcd(4*x,4*x)`, `modgcd(4*x,6*x)`.
