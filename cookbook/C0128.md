# Seeded fuzz targets: libFuzzer's seed pass ignores -max_total_time

- fuzz.yml seeds lang_lower/lang_print/lang_analysis from lang_parse's cached corpus (~69k inputs at 2.2).
- libFuzzer runs every seed once before fuzzing and does not stop at `-max_total_time` during that pass.
- So the job time is (seed count x per-input cost), growing with the seed corpus; lang_analysis's pass took 75 of 120 min before T5 (`Done 69583 runs in 4530 second(s)`) and overran once T5 doubled the per-input cost (Fuzz 46127546893, last line `#65536 exec/s 22`).
- Before adding work per input to a seeded target, read the last run's `Done N runs in S second(s)` line: per-input cost x seeds must stay well under the job's `timeout-minutes`.
- Levers: move the target to the faster assertions group (no ASan), cap per-input query counts, or seed from a smaller corpus.
- Source: 2.2/T5.c1.
