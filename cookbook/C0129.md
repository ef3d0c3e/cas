# cas-lang node ranges hold trailing trivia inside them
- A node's `text_range()` can include whitespace the parser attached before finishing the node: half-typed `f(x) := ` gives a `Definition` of 0..8, not 0..7 (T6.c1, CI 46145814818 `rule_parameters`).
- So a test's expected scope/node range is the node's own range, trivia included; derive it from the tree (`sexp`/`tree` in parser/tests.rs) rather than from the visible text.
- Occurrence ranges (a `Name` node) are tight in practice; scope ranges (Definition, BlockExpr, Lambda) may not be.
