# Gosper with symbolic parameters: dispersion by factor pairs, B-A as one polynomial

Wall (1.3 T6.c2, wz_certificate with parameters a,b,c,n): Gosper's shift search took the
resultant res_k(a(k), b(k+h)) over a and b whole; with four parameters `subres::resultant`
ran >15 min (gdb). Move: factor a and b first and take the resultant per pair of irreducible
factors (crates/cas-core/src/native/sum/gosper.rs `decomposed`): 0.2 s, same shifts.
Second trap: `degree_bound` compared leading coefficients of A and B separately; with parameter
coefficients it returned no bound and Gosper failed. Compute B-A as one polynomial and take its
degree (0 when it vanishes).
Also: WZ certificate without simplifying factorials: R = Factor(Together(y*(r_n-1)*r_k(k-1))),
y Gosper's rational multiplier, r_n = F(n+1,k)/F(n,k), r_k = F(k+1)/F(k) (native/sum/wz.rs).
giac's wz_certificate returns 0 when its sum fails: a sentinel, not a certificate; check any
answer with simplify((F(n+1,k)-F(n,k)-(G(k+1)-G(k)))/F) = 0, G = R*F(n,k-1).
