# giac's epsilon: 1e-12 by default, and its boundary drifts in the shared oracle
Found at T5.c1 (2026-11-23, epsilon2zero/CAS-569).
- libgiac's default `epsilon` is 1e-12 (not 1e-10 as some docs say): `epsilon2zero(1e-11*x)` keeps it; a fresh process zeroes |x| <= 1e-12.
- In the oracle's shared libgiac (crates/cas-giac/src/lib.rs `shared()`), after other rows ran, `epsilon2zero([1e-12,...])` kept 1e-12: the boundary depends on process state.
- Oracle rows stay clear of |x| = eps (use 1e-13 and 1e-11); check `epsilon` in caseval before quoting a threshold.
- Our evaluator merges `1e-13+5` into one decimal before a native sees it: epsilon2zero holds its argument, zeroes small decimals in the held tree, evaluates, zeroes again (native/expressions.rs).
