# Reach-inside wrappers must skip held parts of their own head (unbounded nesting)

Symptom: fuzz panic "recursion limit exceeded" (512) in an integrand whose expression is identically zero somewhere
(e.g. denominator 3−3cos²−3sin² after Bioche's tan substitution).
Mechanism: an op that refuses (Together/Expand with "a denominator is zero") stays held as `Together[r]`; a caller that
wraps parts again (polynomial.rs `inside`, reached from heuristics.rs derivative-divides) builds `Together[Together[r]]`,
which re-evaluates to the same shape and nests until the limit.
Move: in any "apply head H inside the parts" helper, return None (treat as opaque) when the part's head is already H:
it was evaluated and refused. Regression: native/integrate/tests.rs `a_zero_denominator_ends` (T1.c2 777f31e, CAS-638).
Triage: check first whether a recent rule change builds the failing form (grep the callers' arity); here D/nth was not.
