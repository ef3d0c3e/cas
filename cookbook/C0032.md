# Oracle rows on factored or signed rational answers: compare normal(ours - giac)

`Giac::agrees` decides equality through our `Equal`, which does not identify `-(A)/B` with
`(-A)/B` or other factored rearrangements. For answers giac returns factored (wz_certificate,
revert coefficients, partial fractions) write the row as `normal(<ours> - <giac answer>)` = 0
instead of agrees on the bare call (crates/cas-giac/tests/calculus_tools.rs, 1.3 T6.c2).
Process trap: `pkill -f <literal>` from your own command line kills your own shell; a
background binary started with `&` outlives its wrapper: `pgrep -x <binary>` and kill by pid.
