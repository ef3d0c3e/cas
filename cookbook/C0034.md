# giac tabvar/tabsign: what step_func_ (misc.cc:8628) really does

Ported from giac 2.0.0.21 misc.cc for CAS-582 (T7.c2 sympy prototype, 31/31 probes, 2026-11-23).
- tabsign = tabvar with `sign` (mode 2): the row is f itself (not factored), no f'' row, no y= row.
- Reductions before tabulating: periodic f is cut to one period (xmin+T, or [-T/2,T/2] on the whole line);
  even/odd f with xmin == -xmax starts at 0 ONLY when bit 0 is set (tabvar with the f'' row); tabsign and diff=1 keep the line.
- Columns: sing (domain mode 1, kept when in the large domain) ∪ critical points (f'=0, f''=0 numerators) ∪ xmin0/xmax0
  when in the domain ∪ domain endpoints ∪ zeros of abs/sign arguments; sorted, ±inf moved to the ends.
- Between points the sign comes from the midpoint (or the neighbour ±1 at infinity); a midpoint outside the domain gives "X".
  A singular inner point gives two columns with "||" (one-sided limits); equal consecutive columns are then dropped
  (giac's erase-and-skip loop), so tabsign shows one "||" and 1/ln(x^2-1) at ±1 merges.
- f' value at a point whose one-sided limits differ is a pair [l,r]. Row order: x, y'=f', y=f, (y')'.
- Infinitely many critical points (abs(x-1) with f'' row, x+sin(x) unbounded) give undef. Arrows are ↑/↓ in libgiac.
- Labels `y'=(...)`, `y=(...)`, `(y')'` print giac's factored derivative; our parser has no prime notation.
