# giac's piecewise answers in oracle rows: compare branch pairs, judge conditions by sampling

Found at T5.c1 (2026-11-23, CAS-596 bspline piecewise).
- `Giac::value` lowers giac's `piecewise([c1,v1,…])` to a List `{c1, v1, …}`, not `Piecewise`; giac prints `x<1` as
  `1>x` and `x>=0 and x<1` as `((x>=0) and (1>x))`, so a structural compare with ours fails.
- Compare pair by pair: values by normal(ours − giac) (C0032); conditions by their truth at sample points
  (k/6, k = −1…7 for knots in [0,1]) (crates/cas-giac/tests/fits.rs `bspline_piecewise`).
- Exact point rows: pass points as rationals (`[1/2,5/2]`, not `[0.5,2.5]`) so ours stays exact while giac converts to
  doubles anyway; then check ours = closed form and giac within its error (C0006).
