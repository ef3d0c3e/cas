# Surface `=` is `Equation`, not `Equal`, in natives

- The language lowers `a = b` (an equation argument such as `syst2mat([x-y=1],[x,y])`) to `Equation[a, b]`; `Equal` is the comparison head.
- A native that takes equations matches both heads, as solve.rs:109 does: `(Symbol::EQUATION | Symbol::EQUAL, [l, r])`.
- Unit tests written in full form with `Equal[...]` pass while the surface form fails; write at least one unit case with `Equation[...]`.
