# CoefficientList is highest degree first (giac order); tests written blind get it backwards

`CoefficientList[p, x]` (native/polytools.rs:349-360, `cs.reverse()`) gives giac's `symb2poly` order:
leading coefficient first, constant last. Mathematica's habit (constant first) is the wrong one here.
Trap (2.1 T2, CAS-650): a test evaluating a fit by Horner with `.iter().rev().fold(..)` read the list
backwards and failed on CI (fit 1.0036 + 0.920x + 0.750x^2 judged off by 0.19 at x = 1/2).
Under R74 (no local test runs) a hand-derived numeric assert is unverified until CI: prefer a structural
assert (head changed, a Ball coefficient present) or fold forward (`iter().fold(0, |s, c| s*x + c)`).
