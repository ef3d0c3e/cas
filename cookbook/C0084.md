# giac's convolution is a packed complex FFT: its lists carry ~1e-9-relative noise; compare within it

Found at 1.7/T5.c1 (CAS-629, kernel_density list and piecewise rows).
- Mechanism: giac's `_convolution` is `fft_conv` (signalprocessing.cc:2646): both real inputs packed in one complex FFT
  (x = c + i·k, product recovered as Im(ifft(X² − conj(X₋ⱼ)²))/4). Rounding grows with the ratio of the inputs'
  magnitudes (bin counts against kernel weights): small entries lose up to ~3 of giac's 12 printed digits
  (truth 2.65630190852e-07, giac 2.6563019299e-07); symmetric data comes out asymmetric.
- A port of giac's FFT (maple.cc:2067, modpoly.cc:12160-12323) gives errors of the same size but other digits:
  bit-exactness is out of reach; don't chase it.
- Practice: compute the convolution directly (or exactly) and certify it; oracle rows compare each entry within
  giac's noise, an absolute tolerance ~1e-9·max|entry| plus 1.5 units of the 12th digit (C0006), never 12 digits.
- Prototype yardstick: keep the FFT port beside the direct sum and tag rows `fft-noise` when
  |ours − giac| ≤ 10·|giac-port − direct| + 1.5 ulp12 (crates/cas-core/proto/density/proto.py).
