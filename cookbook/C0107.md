# giac's Hilbert transform through our Fourier pair; giac's inert heads need a builtin

Found at 1.8/T6 (T6.c1, CAS-648).
- giac's `hilbert(f,t)` (signalprocessing.cc:4911 hilbtrans) is `ifourier(-i*sign(w)*fourier(f,t,w),w,t)`; port it
  through FourierTransform/InverseFourierTransform, not a table.
- Our ifourier sifts a DiracDelta only when it is a direct factor of a product: `Expand` the `-I Sign[w] F` first,
  else `sign(w)*(delta(w-1)-delta(w+1))` stays.
- A delta derivative at w=0 in F (transforms of t, t^2) is giac's `undef`: map it to Indeterminate.
- Our F of rational functions holds e^(-|w|) forms that ifourier cannot invert (linear exponents only): those rows
  depart (inert `Hilbert`); a residue rule would close them.
- giac's inert answers (`Hilbert(f,t)`, `Fourier(...)`) read back as user symbols `$Hilbert` (lower.rs:1894, C0098):
  to make a held answer equal giac's, register the inert head as a builtin with its en.txt name.
