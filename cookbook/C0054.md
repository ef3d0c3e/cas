# C0054 — Subresultant gcd: gamma/lc-scale the last remainder before its primitive part
tags: gcd,subresultant,cas-poly,fuzz,timeout,content,performance · date: 2026-11-24 · phase/task: 1.4/T9 · origin: Fuzz 45304609232, T9.c5 8a82c52

Multivariate subresultant gcd (cas-poly `gcd/subres.rs` `gcd_over`, the poly_gcd fuzz reference): the last
remainder is c·G with c far larger than the inputs (fuzz: degree 174 in z, big integers). Taking its
primitive part by content() = a gcd of every coefficient, each a recursive gcd, cost 7 s.
Fix: Brown's γ normalisation [Bro71]: γ = gcd(lc a, lc b); lc G | γ, so γ·last/lc(last) = (γ/lc G)·G
divides exactly and its leftover factor divides γ (small); the pp step is then cheap (7 s -> 0.26 s).
Keeps subresultants independent of the other gcd methods (it stays the reference).
Triage techniques that worked: time old vs new path behind one env switch in a single build (a fair
before/after); shrink the fuzz case by dropping terms while it stays slow, for a cheap regression test.
