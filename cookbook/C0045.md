# Comparing our balls with giac's intervals (convert(...,interval))

Context: giac prints `convert(e,interval)` as `[lo..hi]` (type DOM_SPECIALFLOAT); ours is a ball (T3.c2, CAS-592, 2026-11-23).
- giac's ends: `left(convert(e,interval))` / `right(...)` work but are rounded to the printed digits; `inf`, `sup`, `lower`, `upper` do not give them.
- Oracle move (crates/cas-giac/tests/interval.rs): regex each `[lo..hi]` out of giac's text, widen by one unit of its last printed digit, pair with our balls in pre-order and require overlap; replace both by symbols `iv<k>` and compare the rest symbolically (`[..]+x` vs ball+x).
- cas-giac cannot name `Ball` (no cas-num dependency): use `View::Ball(b)` then `b.mid().to_rational()`, `b.rad().to_float()?.to_rational()`.
- giac ignores small digit counts: `convert(sqrt(2),interval,5)` is the 53-bit interval; ours a 5-digit ball (they still overlap).
