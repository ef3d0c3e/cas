# Certified KKT points under bounds: one-point ranges, and simple inequalities as bounds

- NLPSolve (crates/cas-core/src/native/optimization/nlpsolve.rs `active`, `optimal`) certifies a search point by fixing the variables at the bounds they meet and checking the sign of the Lagrangian's derivative on the side each sits.
- A variable whose range is one point (lo = hi) has no side: checking it as "at its lower end" rejects true optima. Branch and bound on relaxations (nlpinteger.rs) creates such ranges at every fixed integer, so record no side and skip the check for them (1.5/T4.c2).
- A single-variable linear inequality (`x1 <= 1`, `2x >= 3`) kept as a general inequality costs a multiplier and can defeat certification (giac's own `2-x1…x5/120` row stayed unevaluated). Turn it into a variable bound first, as giac does (optimization.cc:6833-6855; ours `as_bound` in `program`).
