# giac's descending sorts are reversed stable ascending

giac's SortD/sortd (ti89.cc:1706, sortad(v,false)) answer the reverse of the stable ascending order, not a stable
descending sort: ties come out reversed. SortD([1.5,3/2]) = [3/2,1.5]; SortD([[2,1,2],[3,2,1]]) = [[2,2,1],[1,3,2]]
(probes .run/1.6/T8/c2probe*.cas). Port a giac descending sort as `ascending.reverse()`, and probe a tie before
porting any other giac descending order.

Source: 1.6/T8.c2 (crates/cas-core/src/native/lists/ordering.rs).
