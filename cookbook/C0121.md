# C0121 — Oracle sweep where giac is wrong: probe the class with caseval, then skip on an exact predicate
tags: proptest,oracle,sweep,giac,departure,caseval · date: 2026-11-27 · phase/task: 2.1/T7 · origin: CI 46055615575 random_is_irreducible

When a random oracle sweep fails and our answer is the right one (check by hand: factor it, plug in), giac is wrong
on a class of inputs, not one input. Find the class before writing the skip:
- Probe 8-12 hand-built variants in one `PY scripts/caseval.py <file.cas> 30` run: the shrunk input, its factored
  form, the same shape with simpler factors, and near-misses that should not trigger it.
- 2.1 T7 (CAS-660): `is_irreducible(2*(y+1)*(x^2*y-x^2-1))` gave 1. The probes showed giac answers 1 whenever a
  factor in `y` alone exists and `x` occurs (`(y+1)*(x^2+1)`, `(x+1)*(y+1)`, `(y^2+1)*(x+y)`), and is right
  otherwise (`x*(x+y)`, `(y+1)*(y+2)`, `(x+1)*(x*y+2)`). The 1.x skip ("monomial content") was only a special case.
- Skip on an exact predicate of the drawn input (C0116): here the gcd over Q[y] of the x-coefficients, by
  primitive pseudo-remainders on i128 (`y_content`, crates/cas-giac/tests/polytools_three.rs). The test crate has no
  cas-poly dependency; for degree <= 2 a 30-line helper beats adding one.
- Pin the shrunk input as a departure row (giac's printed answer, ours) and widen the README sentence.
