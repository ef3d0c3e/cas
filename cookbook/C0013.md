# Comparing a complex ball with a close value: by parts, never by Abs

`Abs` of a complex ball is sqrt(re² + im²) (cas-num/src/ball/complex.rs:281); when the two values agree the ball under
the root holds 0 and `Ball::sqrt` gives INDETERMINATE (ball/real.rs:373-387), so `Less[Abs[a - b], tol]` never
decides and a closeness check silently says "differ". Compare `g = N[a - b]` by `Less[Abs[Re[g]], tol]` and
`Less[Abs[Im[g]], tol]`: `Re`/`Im` of a complex ball are real balls (native/parts.rs:97-108), a real ball's abs is
`[|m| ± r]`, and non-overlapping balls order definitely. A check that passes only while the gap stays away from 0
(giac's 12 digits vs ours) hides the bug. Exact closed forms that differ only in shape: `Expand[u - v] == 0`.
Example: `near` and `expands_same` in crates/cas-giac/tests/complex_solving.rs (T5.c3).
