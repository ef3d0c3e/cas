# rowan token lookup by offset: covering_element, not token_at_offset

- rowan 0.16.1 `SyntaxNode::token_at_offset` visits every child at every level: linear in a wide node's children (a long Root or ArgList).
- Called at many offsets (a fuzz target sampling 1024 offsets) it went quadratic and stalled the job (Fuzz 46118108617).
- `covering_element(TextRange::new(o, o + 1))` binary-searches each level; with `prev_token()` it gives the left/right tokens at a boundary.
- Walking back over trivia with `prev_token()` is still linear in the trivia run: bound the callers' offset count, not the walk.
- Source: 2.2/T5.c1 cursor.rs (commit 6bfb89b2).
