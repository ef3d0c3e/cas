# Expression shapes a cas-core native meets (steps, deltas, intervals)
Found at T5.c2 (2026-11-23, linstep/simplifyDirac/periodic, CAS-570/572/573).
- `x=a..b` lowers to `Equation[x, Range[a,b]]`; a two-argument Range stays unevaluated (match Equation and Equal, C0016).
- `D` of `DiracDelta` gives `Derivative[1][DiracDelta][x]`; giac's `Dirac(x,n)` lowers to `DiracDelta[x,n]`, which stays: accept both.
- `Abs` does not split products (`abs((x-1)*(x+1))` stays): Factor, then split yourself.
- `Times[-1, Plus[...]]` is not distributed: `HeavisideTheta[(-1-x)/(-1)]` stays; build `x + b/a` directly.
- `ev.eval(Solve[Equation[p,0],x])` gives exact real values, or balls past the exact degree.
