# giac's stored doubles keep 47 fraction bits: floors of decimal products can be one lower

Problem: giac (64-bit build) stores a double in a gen with the low 5 mantissa bits cleared, so every
double it keeps is truncated toward zero to 47 fraction bits. A plain f64 or exact port then disagrees
wherever a floor or comparison sits on an integer: giac's createwav(duration=0.001, samplerate=8000)
makes 1+floor(0.001*8000) = 8 samples (0.001 truncated is just below 1/1000), f64 and exact give 9;
duration=1/1000 too (giac converts it to a truncated double first).

Probe: `(1.0+2.0^(-47))-1.0` gives 7.1e-15 in giac, `(1.0+2.0^(-48))-1.0` gives 0.0,
`(1.0+255*2.0^(-52))-1.0` gives 224 ulp (`scripts/caseval.py`).

Move: in the Python prototype, run both a plain-f64 copy and a 47-bit copy (clear the low 5 bits of
each stored double); rows that differ only there are departures, ours exact on exact input (R67).
Samples, codes and ordinary arithmetic are rarely affected: only floors/ties at integers.

Seen: 1.7/T7.c1 (f3689ae), probe9:40-41 and crates/cas-core/proto/sound/clip/dur.cas rows 4-5.
