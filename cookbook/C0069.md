# giac quoted products are not flat: aplatir_fois_plus multiplies them out in mathml

Probing a printer with quote(...) to fix giac's internal shape (C0067) has a trap in mathml.cc's printer:

- giac's parser builds a quoted product two factors at a time (nested binary `*`), not one n-ary prod.
- mathml.cc's gen2mathml calls `aplatir_fois_plus` (gen.cc :12505) on every symbolic first, which flattens and,
  through sym_mult (:7228), multiplies the numeric factors out: `mathml(quote(2*inv(4)*x))` prints 1/2·x, where
  markup.cc (latex, export_mathml) keeps \frac{2}{4}.
- A prototype's parser must nest quoted products the same way and the port must flatten once at the top, or rows
  that look identical between printers disagree.
- giac's `gen ==` compares numbers across types: exponent 0.5 equals 1/2, so pow(x,0.5) takes the sqrt branch.
