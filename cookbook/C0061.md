# Certified global minimiser on an interval: float candidate, ball cover, interval Newton

Use for any "answer the argmin of f on [a,b]" native where the answer must be a certified ball
(find_minimum, 1.5/T6, crates/cas-core/src/native/optimization/find_minimum.rs).

- A float search (giac's Brent queue) only supplies a candidate and an upper bound U of min f
  (f's ball upper at the candidate and at a, b). It certifies nothing; eps and maxiter act on it.
- Ball cover of [a,b] (minimax.rs's certify pattern): each piece bounded below by the larger of the
  natural form f(I) and the mean value form f(m) + f′(I)·[−h, h]; drop pieces whose lower bound > U,
  lower U from centre values, bisect the rest; group survivors into connected clusters.
- Close a cluster: interior and f″ compiles with f″(X) > 0 and no Abs/Sign/step in f → interval
  Newton N(X) = m − f′(m)/f″(X) ⊆ X proves the one critical point, iterate on N ∩ X; touching an end
  with f′(X) of the right sign → that end's exact ball; else the hull once its width ≤ eps.
- Clusters whose value ball lies wholly above another's are dropped; true ties cannot be separated in
  balls: answer one (the leftmost) with a warning and say in the doc it is certified only as a local
  minimiser whose value lies within the value balls of the minimum.
- Bound the work (C0019): pieces ≤ min(terms/16, 2^16); queue pops × maxiter ≤ terms.
