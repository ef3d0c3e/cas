# giac's kmeans cluster_crit indices: numeric quirks a port must reproduce

From 1.8/T5.c3 (CAS-646), porting optimization.cc:10784-11091 against 609 caseval rows:
- `WG`'s lower triangle is copied from `_T[i][j]`, which stays the identity until `T()` first runs; whether it ran
  depends on which indices were asked for and on det(WG) ≠ 0. Port the call order, not the maths.
- The det ratio comes out exact (e.g. `1630216/3`) when the double det(WG) is exactly 1.0: dividing by a double 1
  keeps giac's exact numerator.
- A double /0.0 prints `infinity` (ours ComplexInfinity); 0/0 is undef → "not defined" in `output=index`.
- giac's float determinant zeroes pivots below 1e-12, then below 1e-12 × the previous pivot.
- `trace-WiB` on a singular W: `infinity` unless W splits into blocks with B zero on the singular block ("not
  defined") — an observed rule, not derived.
- "log-det" maps to the det ratio (name2index); `ncopt` with no suggestion gives k=0 → `[]`.
- Seed-dependent index rows: check each `[k,value]` against the prototype's values at that k over 200 seeds.
