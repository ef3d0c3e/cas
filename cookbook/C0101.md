# Reproducing giac's printed rational forms in our evaluator

From 1.8 T4.c2 (shift_phase, period; crates/cas-core/src/native/leftovers_two.rs):
- Our Together keeps rational coefficients (`x + Pi/2`) and evaluation folds `cos(x + Pi/2)` to `-sin(x)`;
  giac's ratnormal prints `(pi+2*x)/2`, which stays. Build that form by hand: distribute the 2 over the
  sum's terms, never Expand (Expand reaches into nested arguments: shift_phase(sin(sin(x))) folded).
- `Abs[2 Pi/a]` evaluates to `2 Abs[Pi/a]`, never structurally equal to giac's `2*pi/abs(a)`: build
  `n/Abs[d]` directly.
- A giac rewrite that picks its form by expression size (linabs) gives forms ours cannot match: compare
  by value at sample points (C0026).
