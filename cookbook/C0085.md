# giac's rounding decisions taken in doubles: make them in f64 too, even on exact data

Found at 1.7/T5 (CAS-629, CI 45597160314 oracle `lists`).
- Mechanism: an index or branch giac derives from doubles (kernel_density's nearest bin `(int)((x-a)/d+0.5)`,
  optimization.cc:7238) lands on an exact tie for exact data ([1,2,3,2] on [1,3] in 100 bins: 2 sits at 49.5).
  giac's doubles fall just below (49.4999…) and pick bin 49; exact rational arithmetic picks 50, so every entry of
  the answer shifts (ours mirrored giac's list) though both are "right".
- Practice: compute such discrete choices (bin indices, floor/trunc of a ratio, comparisons that pick a branch) in
  f64 with giac's operation order, then do the arithmetic that follows exactly or in balls. Exactness belongs to the
  values, not to giac's conventions.
- Prototype check: a prototype in Python floats reproduces this by accident; probe a tie row (symmetric data, an odd
  grid) to see it.
