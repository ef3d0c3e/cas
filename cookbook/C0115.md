# Sweeping an optimiser against giac: compare the optimum, check our point is feasible

Random small LP/transport problems tie often (several optimal allocations). Comparing giac's allocation fails on ties. Accept: the same answer, or giac's optimal cost with our allocation feasible (row sums = supplies, column sums = demands, entries ≥ 0) and of that cost (1.8 T8.c4 `random_tpsolve`; same for lpsolve: value only, R1.8-004).
Also from T8.c4:
- giac `linear_interpolate` misreads negative abscissae (errors "Values out of range" or wrong values): sweep nonnegative, dyadic abscissae/steps (giac's doubles then land on the last point).
- harness: a `proptest!` body ending in `return Err(..)` trips clippy `unreachable_code` (the macro appends `Ok(())`): write `if !ok { return Err(..) }`.
