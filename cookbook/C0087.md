# giac answers from random starts: measure seed dependence in the prototype first

Problem: giac commands whose result is a local optimum from random starts (kmeans' Hartigan-Wong from
init_cluster_centers' _rand draws) can give a different answer under another generator; ours draws from
our own seeded generator, so giac's row may not be reproducible at all.

Move: in the Python prototype (C0009), run every such probe row under >= 200 seeds of Python's RNG in
place of giac's draws and count the distinct answers (partitions up to relabelling for clusterings).
- one answer under every seed: an oracle row (compare up to relabelling).
- several: a departure/property row: ours must be one of the answers the prototype found (giac's among
  them), and must repeat under SeedRandom (C0014, C0044). Never chase giac's digits.
A row that matched giac only at a non-zero prototype seed is the signal.

Origin: 1.7/T6.c1 CAS-630 (R1.7-012): 9 of 18 kmeans probe rows seed-dependent (E6 with k=3: 157/200).
