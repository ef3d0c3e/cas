# C0055 — Euclid over Z[i] (and other field-backed rings): round the quotient in the ring from leading bits
tags: gcd,gaussian,euclid,cas-num,fuzz,timeout,performance,rounding · date: 2026-11-24 · phase/task: 1.4/T9 · origin: Fuzz 45311134759, T9.c6 8c9ca3e

Euclid in a ring whose division goes through its field (cas-num `complex/ring.rs` `GaussianIntegers::div_rem`):
building the exact quotient a/b as rationals reduces each part with a big-integer gcd as large as the
operands at every Euclid step -> quadratic-plus (rewrite fuzz: TranscendentalSimplify on tan/cos/sin
powers -> heuristic gcd over Z[i], 61 s timeout).
Fix: round inside the ring: q = round(Re/Im(a*conj(b)) / N(b)), halves away from zero (Rational::round's
rule); when b's parts exceed 128 bits and a's are at most 32 bits longer, take the quotient from b's top 64
bits (error < 2^-28), falling back to the exact path within 2^-20 of a half, so q is unchanged. Checked on
20000 random pairs (a quarter near ties) against exact rational rounding. 16000-bit gcd 4.7 s -> 64 ms.
Fuzz replay budget: the fuzz build ran ~17-24x slower than local release here, so aim for < 0.5 s local
release to get a replay < 10 s under CI flags.
