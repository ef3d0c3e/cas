## Answer

- Strings are interned atoms `View::String(&str)` in cas-core. Constructor `Expr::string(&str)`, accessor `Expr::as_str()`. Nothing string-related runs in arithmetic or in list functions unless a native says so.
- cas-core has NO dependency on cas-lang (cas-core/Cargo.toml has no cas-lang entry). A cas-core native cannot reach the printer or the parser. Only cas-giac and the fuzz crate depend on both.
- Pure string ops (char, asc, ord, mid, left, right, inString, contains, trim, split, join, cat, length, levenshtein, hamming) can be cas-core natives: they only need `as_str`, `Expr::string`, `Expr::apply` for lists.
- `string(x)`, `expr(s)`, `latex(x)`, `mathml(x)` need either the cas-lang printer or parser, or a new printer. A new LaTeX/MathML printer could live in cas-core (it prints Expr structure); `string(x)` and `expr(s)` must be cas-lang (or a hook that cas-core does not have).
- cas-lang's Session intercepts only top-level statements whose head is `with_sqrt`/`all_trig_solutions` with one argument (session.rs:286-292). Calls nested in other expressions are not intercepted.
- No giac string names are in en.txt. `Length`, `Sort`, `Join`, `First`, `HammingDistance` exist (en.txt:99, :183, :186, :189, :380).
