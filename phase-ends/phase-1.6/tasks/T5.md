Decisions:
- binding: MathML presentation and content share T4's engine pass (one MarkupBlock, giac's design); mathml.cc's older layout is a separate cas-lang module over the same lowering.
- The default form's annotation holds our input form (`1 + x`, giac `x+1`); the oracle compares everything but the annotation with giac, and the annotation with our `string(x)` escaped.
- mathml's file form (2nd argument a string) and giac's 5000-node guard are not ported: any 2nd argument leaves the call; our caps are the depth check and the length cap.
