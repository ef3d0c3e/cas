## Decisions
- Cap counts bytes (`String::len`), which is >= chars; documented as bytes in the trait.
- The up-front `size() > max_len` refusal (from the brief) can refuse a text that fits: `Times[-1, x]` has size 3 and prints `-x`. Only matters for tiny caps.
