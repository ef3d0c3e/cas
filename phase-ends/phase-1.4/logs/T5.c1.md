## Changed
- `crates/cas-core/src/native/interpolation/splines.rs` (new): `b_spline`, `spline_fit`.
  - core `spline(ev, x, knots, points, p, piecewise)`: per piece, de Boor on unit control points → p+1 weight polys (Rational) → `Expand[Σ w·c]` per column; giac collapse; `piecewise` → one `Piecewise` per column, giac conditions.
  - fit: giac non-GSL path (basis `eval_basis_splines`, clamped knots, `subinterval_index`, `deBoor`), `(AᵀA)Q = AᵀY` by exact Gauss–Jordan over Rational (giac: banded Cholesky in doubles); derive by control-point differences; variable → `spline` on the fit's unpadded knots directly (no unevaluated BSpline leaks).
  - decimals: `Float::to_rational_within(MAX_DECIMAL_BITS = 4096)`; any decimal in data/breakpoints/points → answers `Ball::from_rational` at working precision.
  - caps in module doc: MAX_DEGREE 32, MAX_COLUMNS 64, MAX_SYMBOLIC_CONTROL_POINTS 32, MAX_SPLINE_WORK 2^18 (pieces·(p+1)⁴ and pieces·(p+1)²·columns), MAX_FIT_WORK 2^17 (m·nb², nb²·series, points·(p+1)²·series), MAX_POINTS rows/points/control points.
