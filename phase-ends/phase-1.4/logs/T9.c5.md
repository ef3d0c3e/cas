## Fix (crates/cas-poly/src/gcd/subres.rs `gcd_over`)
- γ = gcd(lc pa, lc pb) (one gcd of input-sized coefficients). lc G | γ (G primitive divides both, Gauss), so `γ·last / lc(last) = (γ/lc G)·G` exactly (div_coeffs); its content divides γ, small; pp of that × d. Brown's γ normalisation [Bro71]. Subresultants stay independent of the other methods (reference).
- Doc: crates/cas-poly/doc/algorithms/gcd.md §Subresultants, new paragraph "The primitive part of the last remainder".
- Regression test: gcd/tests.rs `subresultants_with_a_large_last_content` — greedy term-drop reduction (.run/1.4/t9/c5/minimize.py) of the unit: f without −20x²y³, h without 8x³y³z³. Same build with a temporary OLD switch: old 1.15–1.18 s release, new 12–13 ms release, 0.18 s debug (3 runs each).
