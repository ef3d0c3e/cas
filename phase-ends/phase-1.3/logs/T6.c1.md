## Port notes
- ibpdv/ibpu share `step()`: `[f,w]`, `[f,w,x]`, `[f,w,x,a,b]`; `f` may be `[F,r]` (pair of 2 only, else stays); default var `x`; giac's `x=a..b` form not taken (no interval syntax needed).
- giac `normal` → our `Together`.
- preval: substitution first, one-sided `Limit` (sides from inside [a,b], giac's) only when a substituted value has Indeterminate/Infinity/ComplexInfinity/DirectedInfinity. giac always uses limits; ours by series leaves some calls (e.g. a staying `Integrate`) as they are. `preval(f, x=a, b)` accepted as giac.
- function_diff: `g` applied to private `FunctionDiff$t` (Plus/Times/Power through operands, numbers kept, else `g[t]`), `D`, then a `Derivative` or `D` left → call stays (giac's `lop(dg,at_derive)`). Parameter `x`, or `x1…x1000` if the derivative has `x`, then `FunctionDiff$x`.
- Lists: our `Power[List, -1]` does not thread (giac's `1/[x,y]` does), so when the derivative at `List[p,q]` is not `List[d(p), d(q)]` the result is `Function[List[x], If[ListQ[x], Map[Function[List[x], body], x], body]]`; else plain `Function[List[x], body]` (sin → `x -> cos(x)`).
