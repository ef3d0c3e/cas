## Decisions
- `search` takes an extra `max_pops` (pops budget = terms/maxiter, C0019); `Stop::Limits` past it, `Stop::NotReal` at a non-finite value.
- Candidate NaN (`Stop::NotReal`) does not end the call: the cover alone decides (U from a, b, centres). Interrupt during search: f returns NaN, then `interrupted(ev)?`.
- Krawczyk/Newton only when body has no Abs/Sign/HeavisideTheta (differentiability) and f″(X)>0 (strict local min).
- Closed clusters compared by value balls: drop those whose lower end > least upper end; >1 left → leftmost + tie warning.
- Warnings: "find_minimum: past the limits" (maxiter > terms, pops over); "find_minimum: no certified minimiser within the limits" (cap pieces min(terms/16, 2^16), or nothing closed); the tie text from the brief.
- Answer `Expr::from(ball)`; end answers are the ball of a or b (exact for rational ends).
- Oracle inputs spelled `x^2-2*x`, `x->x^4-3*x^2+x` (giac same answers as probe3: caseval .run/1.5/giac/probe_t6c1b.cas); closed form of the quartic root `sqrt(2)*cos(acos(-sqrt(2)/4)/3-4*pi/3)` = −1.3008395659415771262 (giac evalf).
- Python prototype of search (.run/1.5/t6c1/proto.py): cosh→−1.4e-8, x²−2x→1, quartic→−1.3008395655, sin 0..10→4.71238898, sin 0..4π→10.9956 (7π/2; the cover then picks the leftmost 3π/2 with the tie warning).
