## Departure
- Cut added as the GMI cut itself (Σ g x_N ≥ 1); giac appends the integralized coefficients with rhs 1 (lpsolve.cc:762-786), a different inequality when the scale ≠ 1 that may cut off integer points. Integralized form used only for the 1e6 magnitude test.
- giac's simplex break on its iteration limit inside a child leaves a partial child that may still be processed; ours stops the search at once (incumbent or {}).
