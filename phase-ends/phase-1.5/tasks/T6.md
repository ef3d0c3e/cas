Done: `find_minimum` answers a ball certified to hold a minimiser of f on [a,b]; `frank_wolfe` answers the iterate whose Frank–Wolfe gap is certified below tol; both registered, documented, unit and oracle tested; CI green on 4715ab0.
Summary: Two giac minimisers now exist. find_minimum searches like giac (Brent over a queue of intervals) but then proves its answer with ball arithmetic, so the answer is an enclosure of the true minimiser rather than a float. frank_wolfe minimises a function over the convex hull of a list of points like giac does, but only stops once the optimality gap is proven below the tolerance, which bounds how far its value is from the minimum for convex f.
