## Exclusions / departures (caseval probes)
- Strings ending in `\`: giac's lexer reads `"a\\"` as unclosed (`size("a\\")` = undef). Skipped.
- giac prints `"` in a string doubled and `\` bare (`"a\"b"` → `"a""b"`): not readable back; string results compared through `asc`; split's strings drawn without `"` / `\`.
- ord("") and ord of lists with "": giac error (existing departure); skipped.
- contains("",""): giac 0 (its inString("","") 0, contains("abc","") 1), ours 1. New departure, excluded. CI 45824175835 shrink: s="", other="".
- mixdown of two 8-bit clips: giac garbage near -128 (codes [1,-127,5] + [1,9] at 0 → giac [-123,-117,-121], expected [2,-118,5]). Departure; depth 8 out of the mixdown sweep.
- splice crossfade longer than a clip: giac heap overflow, aborts the process (CI 45824175835 sound binary SIGABRT "corrupted size vs. prev_size"; locally stereo 10+2 samples len 6 → "free(): invalid next size"; mono 16+1 len 6 too). Length clamped to min(both lengths). Note: existing values row `splice(a,createwav([0.9]),3,1)` has crossfade 3 > 1 sample: same risk class.
- No sweep of resample, createwav options.
