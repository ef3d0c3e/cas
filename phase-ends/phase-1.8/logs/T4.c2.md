## Decisions
- period: own trig2exp (giac's tan as (e^{2iu}-1)/(i(e^{2iu}+1)); our TrigToExp gives e^{±iu}, period 2pi/3 for tan(3x), wrong).
  `Abs` and non-integer powers -> not periodic (giac's abs(sin x) = +infinity). floor check decides alone
  (period(floor(x)-x) = 1, x outside allowed). Unit `2*Pi/Abs[c]` (Abs[2Pi/a] evals to 2 Abs[Pi/a], Equal undecided vs giac).
- linabs: same values as giac, form may differ (size heuristic; sin(x)*abs(x)/x vs giac sin(abs(x))): oracle compares values.
  Splits skip unselected-factor products >1 (giac takes only the first factor: unsound); bounds MAX_SPLIT 4 (giac 10).
- lin bounds: MAX_POWER 32, MAX_TERMS 512 (fuzz).
- shift_phase argument built as (2u+pi)/2 with 2 distributed over the sum only: our Together keeps x+Pi/2 (rational
  coefficients), which evaluation folds back to sin(x); Expand reaches into nested shifts and folds them.
- envelope: Solve {F=0, dF/dt=0} in x,y -> [x(t),y(t)] (list of them if several); accepts Equation (`=` lowers to Equation, not Equal).

## Gotchas (generalizable)
- Our Together keeps rational coefficients (x + Pi/2, not (2x+pi)/2); a giac form over one denominator must be built.
- giac `=` lowers to `Equation`, not `Equal`, in natives.
- Abs[2 Pi/a] evaluates to 2 Abs[Pi/a]: Equal with giac's 2*pi/abs(a) undecided; build c/Abs[d].
