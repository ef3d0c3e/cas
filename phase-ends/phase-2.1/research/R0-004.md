(b) Methods
- Tupper, "Reliable two-dimensional graphing methods for mathematical formulae with two free variables", SIGGRAPH 2001, pp. 77-86 (ACM). Abstract (search snippet, from history.siggraph.org and a Springer entry): series of algorithms for reliably graphing 2-D implicit equations and inequalities; a naive standard interval arithmetic approach fails, and later algorithms extend interval arithmetic. Full text not fetched. Tupper's 1996 MSc thesis "Graphing Equations with Generalized Interval Arithmetic" (U Toronto) is the related source; the fetched thesis page was only the introduction to section 4.7.
- Wikipedia (Tupper's self-referential formula, fetched 2026-11-27): the formula is an inequality decoding a bitmap; it cites the 2001 paper as "reliable two-dimensional graphing algorithms" tied to GrafEq. No algorithm detail.
- Marching squares, adaptive sampling with pole/discontinuity detection: general knowledge, not sourced in this run.
- Xcas, SymPy, Maxima, GeoGebra, Desmos one-liners: NOT verified here. Unverified recollection only: SymPy plot_implicit uses adaptive interval-based subdivision; Maxima plot2d uses adaptive sampling over gnuplot; Desmos and GeoGebra internals are not publicly documented in detail.
