Decisions:
- Every edit answers a new clip (giac mutates its argument); arithmetic in f64 in giac's order; giac's decode→encode shrink (2^(b-1) vs 2^(b-1)-1) kept.
- Errors are warnings and the call stays; giac's generic type/dimension errors stay silently.
- splice: clips of other depth, rate or channels always refused ("Clips should have …"), giac only checks when the exponent is automatic: departure rows. Short second clip: giac's truncated answer ported with bounded copies.
- Clipping: one warning per call when any sample clips (giac: only the last sample): departure row.
- resample q0/q1: libsamplerate's linear and ZOH with their one-frame lag (y_k = X(k·sr/nsr − 1)), exact in i128 on the codes, encode exact; len 1 gives a constant (libsamplerate gives nothing).
- resample q2-q4: centred Kaiser-windowed sinc, zeros outside, cutoff 0.90/0.95/0.98 of the lower Nyquist, β 10.06/12.26/14.91, H 37/86/312, L 512/2048/8192 tables (OnceLock, q4 20 MB on first use); measured passband 8.5e-6/9.2e-7/5.4e-8, stopband 102.0/121.7/146.6 dB.
- Limits: rate and output samples ≤ terms, convolution work ≤ 1024·terms, interrupt polled every 1024 outputs; mixdown offsets in seconds saturate, checked before allocation.
Deviations:
- c2 changed the expert's design numbers (H 29/74/232, L 256/1024/4096) after measuring that they missed the passband (1.7-1.9 δ) and q3/q4 stopbands; within the done-when (our own Kaiser tables).
Findings:
- libsamplerate's linear and zero-order-hold converters lag one input frame (src_linear.c:86-132, src_zoh.c:84-120).
- giac splice: a NaN similarity (silent or negatively correlated overlap) encodes every crossfade sample to 0 except the last (pow(1, NaN) = 1); is_audio_position accepts 0.0.
- probe1:42 (splice with an 8-bit second clip, p = 1): giac reads past a buffer; no oracle row.
- resample departures: giac answers "libsamplerate is required for resampling audio"; property rows check ours.
