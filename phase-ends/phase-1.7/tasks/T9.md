Decisions:
- Names: DiscreteWaveletTransform (dwt), InverseDiscreteWaveletTransform (idwt), InstantaneousFrequency (instfreq), InstantaneousPhase (instphase), EmpiricalModeDecomposition (emd); hilbert's list form is a branch of the HilbertMatrix native (en.txt maps hilbert there).
- Haar on exact data answers exact a + b·sqrt(2); other wavelets answer balls with GSL's decimal literals taken as exact rationals.
- giac's centred option is the bare word `center` (also `centre` accepted, a departure: giac rejects bare `centre`).
- emd decides extrema and stopping on f64 like giac, answers balls (each IMF's last sift recomputed in balls, C0064); sifts × n bounded by Limits::terms.
- instfreq/instphase of decimal or ball samples evaluate N[] of each angle (Arg of a complex ball is symbolic atan + Pi).
