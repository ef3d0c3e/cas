## Answer

Most giac leftovers are thin wrappers over code that exists: the gcd algorithms (heuristic, Brown, subresultant, Zippel), `content`, `det` (Bareiss), `dfc`/`dfc2f`, `%`-modular forms, and `csolve`. Missing: the giac surface names `heugcd`, `modgcd`, `psrgcd`, `ezgcd`, `icontent`, `cpartfrac`, `parfrac`, `fullparfrac`, `cfsolve`, `det_minor`, `confrac`, and any complex-field partial fractions. Also missing: an EZ-GCD algorithm, a complex-root partial-fraction split, and a convert option dispatch (`convert` takes only `base`).
