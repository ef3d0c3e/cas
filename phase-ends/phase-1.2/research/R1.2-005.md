## Answer

- Real root isolation by Descartes, VCA/VAS, Fujiwara, Cauchy or Lagrange bounds, or LMQ bounds: NOT FOUND. No roots or isolation module exists in `crates/cas-poly/src` (glob). The only real-root machinery is Sturm's count.
- Sturm is the only exact real-root tool: `DenseRing<Rationals>::count_real_roots` (`crates/cas-poly/src/sturm.rs:92`). It counts distinct real roots, optionally in `[lo, hi]`, with `None` for an infinite end. It gives the counts only, not isolating intervals. `sturmab` is `CountRoots` (`en.txt:172`).
- Real root values (`solve`, `FSolve`) come from bisection in `fsolve.rs`, not Sturm. Complex roots come from `cas-num` balls (`ball/roots.rs:63`). The Krawczyk check is real only.
- Complex root isolation exists: `cas_num::ball::polynomial_roots(coeffs: &[Complex], bits, max_bits) -> Option<Vec<ComplexBall>>` (`crates/cas-num/src/ball/roots.rs:63`). It is used by `proot`.
- Trig solve returns principal solutions only. No integer-parameter convention exists for trig solutions.
- Digits is a read-only builtin value. The working precision lives in `Context`.
- No `all_trig_solutions`, `with_sqrt`, `surd`, `CubeRoot`, `root`, `cfsolve`, `zeros`, `cZeros`, `complexroot`, `rationalroot` or `crationalroot` name exists in `en.txt`.
