## Answer

cas-lang gives a lossless rowan tree with error lists, typed-less lexer tokens, lowering with byte spans and fixes, a session that runs whole texts, and a name table. It gives no line/column or UTF-16 conversion, no token-at-offset, no scope or definition query, no per-builtin docs, and no compile-time Send check (Send is inferred only).
