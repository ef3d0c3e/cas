## Findings

1. Interpolation (crates/cas-core/src/native/interpolation.rs)
- :1-12 InterpolatingPolynomial: Newton divided differences, exact, nested form; forms (xs,ys), (xs,ys,t), ([xs,ys]), Mathematica ({{x,y},...}, t). Variable x by default.
- :13-22 Spline[xs, ys, t, d] (CAS-329): natural-type spline of degree d>=1 through n+1 increasing abscissas; n pieces s_i = sum_k a_{i,k}(t-x_i)^k; end conditions copied from giac for d=2..5; coefficients solved by one linear system via `super::solve::linear_system` (:44). Exact. Output form (Piecewise vs list of pieces) not verified; ev.step at :230.
- :23-33 ThieleInterpolation (CAS-330): continued fraction with inverse differences, exact; step at :276.
- :35-37 Limits: lists of unequal length, repeated abscissa, non-increasing abscissas leave the call. MAX_POINTS = 256 (:53).
- Pade: en.txt:260 only; no native located. NOT FOUND.

2. Regression (crates/cas-core/src/native/statistics/regression.rs)
- :1-21 CAS-297. Exact least squares: PolynomialRegression solves normal equations (V^T V) c = V^T y (:8-9), via `inverse_of` from matrix (:28). Exact rationals. Singular (fewer than n+1 distinct x) leaves the call (:10-11).
- Exponential, Logarithmic, Power regressions are linear regressions on transformed data (:12-21), logs expanded over prime factors with Pollard rho (:38-41).
- Reuse for fitpoly: the Vandermonde normal-equation path is directly reusable; for minimax or a QR path, nothing found.
