- Both-exact 1 passes in cas-core: a-b=0, Abs(N(1))=1, Max(1,1)=1, Less(0, tol*1) is exact, decided TRUE. A failure at exactly 1 therefore needs b to be an inexact ball (radius > 0 containing 1), not an exact 1.
- Inexact ball straddling 1: Max(1,b) stays unevaluated. Then scale is symbolic, tol*scale is not numeric, and Less stays unevaluated, so `near` (which needs Symbol::TRUE) returns false.
- Less(ball [0,r], 10^-10) with r < 10^-10 is TRUE (disjoint, ordered by midpoint). Less(ball, unevaluated Max(...)) stays unevaluated.
