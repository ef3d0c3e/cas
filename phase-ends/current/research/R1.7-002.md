## Answer

- Clip ops (a): stereo2mono is plain channel mean then 16-bit requantise; set_channel_data truncates data past the end; splice crossfade is `((i+1)/cflen)^e` with e=0 meaning plain sum; mixdown uses constant-power pan and squared-cosine fades. Port size about 450 Rust lines plus a resampler dependency.
- Wavelets (b): dwt/idwt are thin wrappers over GSL (external, not in this file). Defaults are Daubechies k=4, pad to next power of 2 with a logged warning. Port size 300 to 700 lines, mostly the GSL filter tables and the 2-D path.
- emd/instfreq/instphase (c): instfreq and instphase on vectors are short, expression forms need symbolic diff/integrate. emd's sifting core was not read (out of budget), so its spline and stop rule are unverified.
