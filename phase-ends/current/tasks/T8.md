Decisions:
- rowNorm rownorm and colNorm colnorm are aliases on LInfinityNorm and L1Norm (giac's linfnorm/l1norm of a matrix are the same sums); colspace is the transpose of image.
- rowspace, colspace, ibasis: reduced echelon rows; giac gives fraction-free multiples ([[-10,0],[0,-10]] for rowspace([[2,3],[4,1]])); oracle compares RowReduce of both.
- syst2mat: coefficient = D[f, v], constant = f at vars → 0, as giac's (`[[y,x,-1]]` for x*y=1).
- matrix(p,q,f) applies only a `Function` head; a symbol naming a user function is a constant (c1's choice, accepted).
- Departures (README): row/col of a range give a list (giac a sequence); negative indices count from the end everywhere (giac errs in mRow); makemat/list2mat with 0 size, reversed subMat bounds, rowDim of a vector, JordanBlock(a,0), non-square BlockDiagonal blocks stay; rowspace(A,d) stays; ibasis with no intersection is {} and rownorm/colnorm of a vector answer, where giac errs.
