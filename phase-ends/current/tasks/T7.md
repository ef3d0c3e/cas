Done: `MinimaxApproximation` (giac `minimax`) answers [error ball, p] or [error ball, p/q] by Remez with a certified error; CI green on pushed head 1ff9731; CAS-599 done.
Summary: The engine now computes the best uniform polynomial or rational approximation of a function on an interval. It runs Remez's exchange until the error levels out to about one part in a million, where giac stops at 2%. The coefficients are exact numbers, and the error is an interval proved to contain the true maximum error of that answer. On several inputs giac's answer is not the best, and ours is visibly better (for example an error of 0.032 where giac gives 0.095); those cases are recorded as departures.
