Done: `cas_lang::analysis::lines` (`LineIndex`, `Position`, `Encoding`) per CAS-DOC-60 §1/§2 with unit tests and a proptest; fuzz target `lang_analysis` wired into fuzz/Cargo.toml, fuzz.yml (seeded from lang_parse) and fuzz/README.md; CI 46102707696 and Fuzz 46102707764 green on 5c16375f.
Summary: Editors count positions as a line and a column, while our parser and queries speak byte offsets. The new line index converts between the two in both counting schemes editors use (UTF-8 bytes and UTF-16 units), with the protocol's line breaks and safe rounding for positions that fall past a line's end or inside a character. A new fuzz target checks those conversions on arbitrary text and is where the next tasks add their own checks.
