## Decisions
- reader validates discrete weights (rational or real ball, ≥0, one >0) so the randvar fixed point is `variable(e).is_some()`; weights like exp(-1) (exact non-rational) are refused "Bad Argument Value" (giac accepts; R67 exact).
- Binomial object needs n, p numbers or symbols.
- Weibull: `FSolve[Equation[f,0], RandomVariable$k, max(1,1/v), NewtonSolver]`, giac's unsquared equation.
- randvar(law) alone for laws other than normal/uniform/exp, randvar(x,…) with an unknown first symbol, randvar(multinomial): stay silently.
- giac's `samples` integer (randvar(f, range, n)) not ported: "Invalid distribution specification".
- density width > Limits::terms → "too many weights".
- `X + X` folds to `2 X` before sample sees it (one draw doubled); giac keeps two.
