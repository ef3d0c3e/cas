## Design
- Systems built by linearity: columns `L(x^i)` (case 1, 2 operators; case 3 recurrence chain per monomial, P reset per family) over an LCM denominator, CoefficientList; Gauss-Jordan over Expr (zero test: number or Simplify), free unknowns 0.
- Poles: FactorList over Q; linear/quadratic, rational coefficients only (else stays); `r(x−c)^m` built by dividing by the other root's factor (Together cannot cancel algebraic roots); Laurent coefficients by derivatives at c; order by Less (reals asc, complex by (re, im)).
- Infinity series: power-series division of reversed coefficient lists; sqrt series by `q = √(g/g0)` recurrence × `√g0`.
- Real filter (:1862): rational, or N[v] a real atom (Int/Integer/Rational/Ball).
- Answer: E^u mapped to Exp[u] (giac's exp reads back as Exp; E^u failed `agrees`).
- Errors: Bad Argument Value → call stays with warning (refuse pattern, number_theory.rs:88).
