## Decisions
- vas_positive and vas take the square-free part (termination). The root 0 is not stripped in vas_positive (matches giac: VAS_positive(x³−3x²+2x) = [1,[1,3]]); variations ignore zeros (giac returns −1 on a leading zero, reached only there).
- sturmseq sign rule (giac's sqff not reproduced): a negative primitive part's sign goes on the odd factor of highest multiplicity; with no odd factor, a constant −1 factor (sequence [-1,0]) first. Matches giac on 14 of 15 probes.
- Rational root order: per multiplicity group (ascending), real roots descending, then non-real by (re, im). Matches the brief's examples and 7 of 11 giac probes; giac's order comes from its linear-factor search (do_linearfind), not cheap.
- SturmSequence with bounds: rational bounds only; giac errors on −inf/inf (`Unable to check sign`); ours leaves the call. Zero numerator leaves the call.
- ComplexRoots: eps rational only (as RealRoots); rectangle keeps balls that meet it (Ball::cmp not definitely outside).

## Departures (in tests/real_roots.rs `departures`)
- VAS_positive((x-1)^2*(x-2)): giac [[0,1],1,[1,4]], ours [1,[1,4]].
- sturmseq(-(x-1)^2*(x+2)^3*(x-5)^4): giac [1,[-1,0],[[1,2],1],1], ours [1,[[-1,-2],-1],1].
- rationalroot/crationalroot order on 4 inputs (listed with both answers).
- Not tested as rows: complexroot with a decimal eps (ours leaves the call), sturm with infinite bounds (giac errors).
- LMQ: exact rounding may differ from giac's doubles at an exact rounding edge; none of giac's examples hit one.
