## Decisions
- Everything algebraic goes through ev.eval: D (linearity test, as laplace.rs `linear`), Expand, Together, Factor,
  Solve (real zeros for the delta split), Simplify (giac's simpfunc), Identifiers (lname).
- Fuzz bounds (giac has none): MAX_BREAKS 16 abs zeros per round, MAX_PIECES 64 intervals over all rounds,
  MAX_DIRAC_ORDER 32, MAX_SPLIT_DEGREE 8 (Exponent of numerator), pole order ≤ 8, rewrite loops ≤ 64 rounds.
- Quadratic step: giac's `boxcar(x1,x2,x)` for a<0 has x1 > x2 → minus the box (verified:
  `linstep(Heaviside(1-x^2),x)` = `-Heaviside(x+1)+Heaviside(x-1)`). Ours is the true box → departure row.
- Pole at the delta: only giac's pure monomial c/(x−z)^k rule (`δ(x)/x` = `-Dirac(x,1)` agrees); other poles
  (e.g. sin(x)/x) leave the term (giac would Taylor-expand).
- periodic with period not known > 0: call stays (giac `Bad Argument Type`) → departure rows.
- Not ported: giac's assumptions on x in simplify_Dirac (x taken real), re/im split of coefficients.

## Traps
- `HeavisideTheta[(-1-x)/(-1)]` does not simplify to x+1 (Times[-1, Plus] not distributed): build `x + b/a` directly.
- Our Abs does not split products: `abs((x-1)*(x+1))` stays; factor_abs splits after Factor itself.
- `D[Dirac(x),x]` is `Derivative[1][DiracDelta][x]`; giac's `Dirac(x,n)` lowers to `DiracDelta[x,n]` (stays).
- `x=a..b` is `Equation[x, Range[a,b]]`; Range of two args stays unevaluated.
