## For the expert
- giac surprise: `srand(42);randvector(20,multinomial,[1/2,1/3,1/6])` => `42,[13,4,3]`: without V giac's randvector gives the COUNTS per index (length len(P), sum n), not n indices; with V it gives n elements (`[c,b,a]`). `frequencies` asserts giac's shape on both sides, so c1's randvector(n,multinomial,P) must answer counts.
- `randvar(multinomial,[1/2,1/2])` ours form assumed `randvar([1/2,1/2])` (by analogy with multinomial,[P],[V] → randvar([w],[v])); c1 must match or the row changes.
- Warning messages assumed: exactly giac's sentence ("Standard deviation must be positive", "Items and weights sizes do not match", …) or "Bad Argument Value" for giac's `call | Error:` form.
- `string(randvar(normal))` departure compares ours with ours of `string(normald(0,1))`, not a literal (our printer's spacing).
- Assumed fixpoint: `randvar([3,1,2,5],[alpha,beta,gamma,delta])` evaluates to itself.
