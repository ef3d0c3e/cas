## Deviation
- In a block, `Fill` stays MisplacedForm (as purge/assume): statement_form runs at top level only. Doing as
  `definition` does in a block would make A a local (assigned_names, free fn without the names table) and then the
  read of A in `FillValue[v, A]` fails ReadBeforeAssignment; needs design, not invented.
